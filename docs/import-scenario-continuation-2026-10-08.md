executes its complete native test driver and exports an isolated archive on an
actual ARM64 Mac. Relevant pushes to `main` use a hosted `macos-15` runner; manual

claim a Mac build or signature verification. Apple Developer ID signing,
notarization, installation and Mac gameplay acceptance remain separate results.

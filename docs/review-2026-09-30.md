# Follow-up review — 30 September 2026

Reviewed the clean `9e8f130` baseline after the tabletop, tempo/save/PWA and Dexter/Isabelle commits. Its 199 tests passed. Both new 33-card starters match the cached official 2026 rulebook, pp. 28–29. The review retains the first-scenario scope and physical-table design.

## Corrections

| Trigger | Corrected behavior |
| --- | --- |
| A teammate commits a skill that rewards a draw | The committer draws, resolves their own weakness, and returns focus to the tested investigator. |
| Twin .45s enters occupied hands; a third spell enters play | Hand slots count two-handed assets correctly; investigators have two Arcane slots, including Necronomicon occupancy in the threat area. Costs are paid once while replacement choices resolve. |
| Twin .45s defeats the first target | Its second attack can select another enemy at the location; the original attack finishes before the next test. |
| Cosmic Flame reveals a skull; Second Sight reveals a cultist | The spell drawback resolves at token timing before the final result and charge-dependent bonuses. |
| Soul Link or Isabelle's recovered skill has an additional horror cost | Horror can be assigned to eligible assets; defeat resolves before the recovered card continues. Isabelle also respects the one-copy commitment restriction. |
| Lucky Charm moves damage; Cloak takes its final horror | Damaged enemies are eligible sources for Charm. Cloak offers its “when” interrupt before destruction. |
| Will of the Cosmos places doom | Choose the player's investigator/asset at the location; count that doom with agenda doom at the normal check. Leaving play removes its doom; agenda advancement clears it. Tokens, total and source breakdown are visible and saveable. |
| Undo follows a draw, search, shuffle, revealed location or story change | Earlier undo snapshots are cleared before the player can hide newly learned information. Public resource actions remain undoable. |
| Setup reaches three selected investigators | Unselected seats are disabled; the UI cannot silently select investigators the engine would omit. |
| A tutorial test, event or reaction opens | Tutorial progress remains mounted; the test recap is visible afterward and advances with Next. Touch focus does not open an obstructive keyboard card preview. |
| Slot metadata is damaged; import runs out of slots/storage | Recover metadata from valid payloads. Preserve the open case and previous saved payload on failure. Corrupt legacy data does not conceal a healthy active slot. Autosave failures are reported. |
| An app update arrives while a case is open | Wait for Save & update; preserve its current checkpoint, including pending tests. Other game tabs choose their own reload. Cached artwork remains available offline and revalidates online. |
